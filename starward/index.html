Starward Empires

A turn-based 4X of exploration, empire and war among the stars.

iOS Android Desktop Coming soon

Starward Empires is a turn-based 4X strategy game in the tradition of Master of Orion and Space Empires. Take an empire from a single homeworld out into a galaxy that waits in the dark, and see how far among the stars you can carry it.

Explore what lies past the next star, settle the worlds your people can call home, and build the fleet that outlasts your rivals — on your phone or at your desk, with the same galaxy waiting on both. Cross-platform multiplayer is on the way.

Starward Empires is in active development. A dedicated site is on the way.
